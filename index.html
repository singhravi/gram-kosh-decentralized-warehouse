About the Project

Built from the ground up — literally.

Gram Kosh is a village-level grain storage unit constructed using local materials — earth, lime, sand, stone, and water — on underutilized Gram Panchayat land, with full community participation.

🏗️
Local Materials, Local Labor
Constructed using earth, lime, sand, stone, and water sourced within the village. Skilled local labor ensures community ownership and employment.
📍
Gram Panchayat Land
Built on underutilized Panchayat land, ensuring zero additional land acquisition costs and direct accountability to local governance.
🌾
Multi-Crop Storage
Designed for paddy, wheat, maize, pulses, and other grains — accommodating the diverse agricultural output of Indian villages.

Engineering Features

5-Layer Grain Protection

Every structural and material choice in Gram Kosh addresses a specific threat to stored grain.

🌡️
Thermal Comfort
Earth and lime walls maintain stable internal temperatures, preventing heat-induced spoilage of stored grains throughout seasonal extremes.
💧
Moisture Control
Strategic ventilation and hygroscopic wall materials regulate humidity, eliminating the moisture conditions that promote fungal growth.
🪲
Pest Protection
Sealed entry points and non-porous lime-finished surfaces deny rodents and insects the crevices they need to establish colonies.
🔥
Fire Resistance
Non-combustible masonry construction with lime plaster provides inherent fire resistance, protecting the grain store and surrounding structures.
⛈️
Weather Resilience
Sloped roofing and elevated flooring guard against monsoon flooding and extreme weather events, ensuring year-round storage integrity.

Gram Panchayat

Governance at the Core

The Gram Panchayat sits at the center of every decision, ensuring democratic accountability and community alignment.

🗺️
01 / Planning
Site & Capacity Planning
Gram Panchayat leads needs assessment to determine optimal location and storage capacity based on local agricultural output patterns and farmer requirements.
🏗️
02 / Construction
Quality Build Management
Ensures local participation, oversees material quality, and coordinates skilled and unskilled labor from within the village community.
🏢
03 / Operation
Daily Storage Operations
Manages grain receipt, storage allocation, and maintains detailed record-keeping systems accessible to all participating farmers.
🔧
04 / Maintenance
Upkeep & Repairs
Coordinates routine inspections and schedules timely repairs to maintain storage quality, structural integrity, and pest-free conditions.
📈
05 / Monitoring
Transparency & Audit
Tracks storage performance metrics, maintains public registers, and conducts periodic social audits to ensure accountability to all village members.
🤝
06 / Benefit Sharing
Equitable Distribution
Implements fair access policies ensuring small and marginal farmers receive proportionate benefits and income from the shared storage infrastructure.
